The user guide explains tasks and results for members and workspace administrators by feature area.

## Reading by role

- Members start with [Basics](/docs/en/guide/basics/getting-started/) for messages, customer conversations, and personal settings.
- Workspace administrators manage [Members and teams](/docs/en/guide/workspace/members/), [Roles and permissions](/docs/en/guide/workspace/roles/), and [Model services](/docs/en/guide/workspace/models/).
- Deployment administrators use [Deployment](/docs/en/deployment/) to manage accounts, workspaces, configuration, and runtime status.

## How the guide is organized

The sidebar groups basics, daily work, AI employees, workspace administration, and personal settings and clients. See [Integrations](/docs/en/integrations/) for channels and business systems.
