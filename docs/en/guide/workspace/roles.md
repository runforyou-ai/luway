Control which features members can use by role. Open it from **Settings → Workspace → Roles and permissions**.

Each member belongs to one role in a workspace, and the permissions the role grants decide which features the member can manage. Changes to a role's permissions or to a member's role take effect immediately, and navigation and settings only show what the member has permission to use.

## Built-in roles

Every workspace comes with three built-in roles:

| Role | Default permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Administrator | All permissions; can't be changed |
| Customer service | Manage external contacts |
| Member | None |

The member who creates the workspace is an **Administrator**. You can change the permissions of **Customer service** and **Member**, but not their names, and you can't delete them. A workspace must keep at least one active administrator.

## Custom roles

On the **Roles and permissions** page, create a role, enter its name and description, and select its permissions. You can edit or delete a custom role at any time; move its members to another role before deleting it. A workspace can have up to 20 roles.

## Administrators

Members with the **Manage workspace** permission can create, edit, and delete roles, and move members to any role except **Administrator**. Only administrators can do things that involve administrators: assign the **Administrator** role, invite administrators, regenerate or revoke administrator invitations, and edit, deactivate, or restore administrators.

## Permission reference

| Permission | Features |
| --- | --- |
| Manage workspace | General settings; invite, edit, deactivate, and restore members, and manage teams; roles and permissions; model services, web search, and business systems; workspace computers; turn other members' personal AI assistants on or off |
| Manage AI employees | Create, edit, deactivate, and reactivate AI employees, maintain evaluations, and view service records; documents and Q&A in knowledge bases; handle knowledge gaps for any AI employee |
| Manage customer service | Channels; business hours, timeouts, conversation summaries, translation, the customer identity secret, service categories, contact fields, and tags |
| Manage external contacts | Create, edit, delete, and restore external contacts, and fill in their contact fields and tags |
| Reports | AI performance and team performance reports |

A permission lets members open the matching pages and make changes; without it, the entry points are hidden. These features aren't limited by role permissions, so every member can use them:

- Messages, direct chats, group chats, and handling conversations in the inbox.
- Colleagues, teams, and external contacts in the directory, personal AI assistants, profile and preferences, and your own computers.
- Viewing knowledge gaps, and handling those for AI employees you are responsible for.
