Connect providers and configure models under **Settings → Integrations → Model services** in your workspace. Connections and models belong to that workspace; other workspaces cannot read or use them.

## Your own model providers

Enter the provider, endpoint and credentials, load or add its model catalog, then select models for AI employees, customer service, translation, summaries and knowledge bases.

## Choosing an available model

Each feature's model selector shows names and sources of models available to the current workspace. Choose a model for the feature and save. After connection or model settings change, test the connection again and check that the selected model is still available.

## Model types

Chat models serve AI employees, summaries and translation; decision models answer classification questions; embedding and rerank models support knowledge indexing and retrieval. Each setting lists models suitable for its purpose. Remove business references before deleting an in-use model.
